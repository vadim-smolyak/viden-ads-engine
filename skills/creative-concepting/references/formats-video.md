# Visual formats — video only (22)

Full entries for the formats named in the `visual-formats.md` selection table. Read that table first — it answers "which format" on its own. Each entry: what it is, awareness fit, what it is best for, the mechanic pairing, and any constraint or production note.

**Awareness shorthand:** `Un` unaware · `Prob` problem-aware · `Sol` solution-aware · `Prod` product-aware · `Most` most-aware · `Any` = a delivery vehicle carrying no stage signal (canon §2). **Mechanic pairings** name only mechanics defined in `creative-mechanics.md`; where no defined mechanic fits the field is absent — that is deliberate, and the mechanic then comes from the concept.

---

### ASMR
**Medium:** Video
**What it is:** Sensory-first video where amplified product sound — pouring, fizzing, crunching, tapping, crinkling — is the hook.
**Awareness:** Un–Sol. Builds product desire through sensation before rational comparison starts.
**Best for:** Food and beverage, skincare, cleaning, supplements — anything where texture or sound is part of the appeal.
**Mechanic:** Implied Answer — the experience argues without a word of copy.
**Note:** Design it to survive muted playback too; the sound is the hook but must not be the only hook.

### Behind the Scenes
**Medium:** Video
**What it is:** Insider access to how the brand actually operates — production line, fulfilment, staff, on set. Access is the hook, not demonstration.
**Awareness:** Un–Sol. Builds affinity with people who have no brand opinion yet.
**Best for:** Brands where craftsmanship, sourcing or ethics is part of the value.
**Mechanic:** Trojan Horse, Social Witness.

### Cinematic B-Roll
**Medium:** Video
**What it is:** Polished, high-production b-roll with deliberate cinematography. Aspirational, not documentary.
**Awareness:** Un–Sol. Builds the brand world before any problem framing.
**Best for:** Premium and lifestyle categories where the visual world *is* the product appeal — fragrance, fashion, home, luxury wellness.
**Mechanic:** Implied Answer — the visuals sell, nothing is stated.

### Demo
**Medium:** Video
**What it is:** The product used in real context to show function or performance. About how it works, not how it looks.
**Awareness:** Sol–Prod. For people evaluating whether it actually does the thing.
**Best for:** Any product with a visible performance moment — tools, cleaning, tech, fitness kit, food prep.
**Mechanic:** Reframe. The demo is the evidence; the mechanic decides what conclusion the viewer draws.
**Note:** This is canon §4's product-in-action type — the hardest to shoot and the highest belief.

### Expert Explainer
**Medium:** Video
**What it is:** An authority figure teaching in a professional capacity — clinician, trainer, scientist, tradesperson.
**Awareness:** Un–Sol. Builds category credibility and educates a market that cannot yet picture the solution.
**Best for:** Health, supplements, skincare, and anything where professional validation lowers perceived risk.
**Mechanic:** Reframe — the expert resets the prospect's understanding before the product appears.
**Constraint:** Credentials must be real and stated accurately.

### Founder
**Medium:** Video
**What it is:** The founder or a named principal speaks directly about the brand, mission or product. The role is stated or unmistakable.
**Awareness:** Un–Sol. Strong for cold acquisition where no brand trust exists.
**Best for:** Mission-led brands, bootstrapped DTC, categories where "who made this and why" is part of the purchase.
**Mechanic:** Trojan Horse, Social Witness.

### Greenscreen
**Medium:** Video
**What it is:** A speaker keyed out and composited over other content — a screenshot, a web page, a graphic, a clip.
**Awareness:** Any. It is a delivery mechanism; the stage comes from the background content and the script.
**Best for:** Reacting to a review, walking a product page, answering a competitor claim, holding up proof while talking.
**Mechanic:** Social Witness, Reframe — the background is the evidence, the speaker supplies the frame.

### How-To
**Medium:** Video
**What it is:** Step-by-step instruction that teaches the viewer to use, make or do something. Numbered steps or explicit teaching language.
**Awareness:** Sol. Aimed at people weighing effort and learning curve.
**Best for:** Products with setup, protocol or ritual — routines, food prep, tools, supplements with a regimen.
**Mechanic:** Reframe ("you have been doing this wrong"), Trojan Horse (genuine instruction that resolves in the product).

### One Shot
**Medium:** Video
**What it is:** A single unbroken clip, usually under ten seconds, carrying one sentence or short paragraph of overlaid text tied to what is on screen.
**Awareness:** Un–Prob. Stillness is itself an interrupt in a feed of hard cuts.
**Best for:** Concepts where the visual is the hook and the copy is the payoff.
**Mechanic:** Implied Answer — the visual asks, the text answers, or the reverse.

### Podcast
**Medium:** Video
**What it is:** Multi-mic setup, two or more speakers, staged conversation or interview.
**Awareness:** Prob–Sol. Conversation reads as earned attention, not purchased attention.
**Best for:** High-consideration products and categories that need nuance before purchase.
**Mechanic:** Reframe, Trojan Horse — the conversation format carries the pitch.

### Reaction
**Medium:** Video
**What it is:** Someone's genuine first impression of a product, result or experience. Unscripted response, not performance.
**Awareness:** Sol–Prod. Somebody else's real experience, for people who need it.
**Best for:** Products with a wow moment — taste, sensation, visible result, surprise value.
**Mechanic:** Social Witness — the reaction *is* the proof.

### Skit
**Medium:** Video
**What it is:** Scripted, staged scenario with characters and dialogue. A mini story with setup and payoff.
**Awareness:** Un–Prob. Entertainment earns the watch and carries the message inside it.
**Best for:** Relatable problem scenarios and pain-first storytelling; humour-forward brands. Strongest when the product resolves the scene.
**Mechanic:** Trojan Horse — the skit is the wrapper, the product is the resolution.

### Social Proof Mashup
**Medium:** Video
**What it is:** Multiple talking-head or native testimonials compiled into one asset. Volume and variety of voices is the hook.
**Awareness:** Prod–Most. Manufactures "everyone is doing this" for people close to buying.
**Best for:** Brands with a deep UGC library. Strongest in retargeting.
**Mechanic:** Social Witness.

### Split Screen
**Medium:** Video
**What it is:** Frame divided deliberately — video one side, text, graphic or second video the other.
**Awareness:** Any. Depends entirely on content.
**Best for:** Concepts needing visual and verbal information simultaneously; objection handling; old way against new way.
**Mechanic:** Contrast Without Comment, Reframe.

### Street Interview
**Medium:** Video
**What it is:** Vox pop — an interviewer asking questions of strangers in a public setting.
**Awareness:** Un–Sol. The public setting signals real people, real opinions.
**Best for:** Categories where a stranger's endorsement persuades. Strongest when the question surfaces a known objection from research.
**Mechanic:** Social Witness, Reframe.

### Testimonial
**Medium:** Video
**What it is:** A customer telling their own experience in first person, to camera or in voice-over.
**Awareness:** Prod. This is canon §2's Product → Offer flow in visual form — lead with proof, not introduction.
**Best for:** High-consideration purchases and categories where "did this work for someone like me" is the conversion question.
**Mechanic:** Social Witness.

### Time Lapse
**Medium:** Video
**What it is:** Accelerated footage showing change across time. Speed of progression is the hook, not just the endpoint.
**Awareness:** Prob–Sol. Progression implies efficacy.
**Best for:** Anything where visible change over time is the proof — skin, hair, plants, spaces, physical training.
**Mechanic:** Implied Answer, Contrast Without Comment.

### Trend
**Medium:** Video
**What it is:** Adopts a currently circulating sound, format or cultural parody. The reference must be recognisable and live.
**Awareness:** Un–Prob. Cultural fluency buys the stop.
**Best for:** Brands with a fast pipeline and an audience that lives in-feed.
**Mechanic:** Trojan Horse — the trend is the wrapper, the message is the payload.
**Constraint:** Short shelf life. Cannot be briefed on normal production lead times.

### Try-On
**Medium:** Video
**What it is:** Creator wears and shows a wearable product — fit, drape, styling, movement.
**Awareness:** Sol–Prod. Nobody buys a wearable without seeing it on a body.
**Best for:** Apparel, footwear, jewellery, eyewear, accessories.
**Mechanic:** Implied Answer, Social Witness.

### Unboxing
**Medium:** Video
**What it is:** Opening and reveal of the packaging as the central event, from receiving to reveal.
**Awareness:** Un–Sol. The reveal structure builds anticipation.
**Best for:** Products where packaging is part of the value; gifting; subscriptions; launches.
**Mechanic:** Implied Answer, Social Witness.

### VSL (Video Sales Letter)
**Medium:** Video
**What it is:** Long-form structured persuasion — typically 60 seconds and up — that introduces and agitates the problem, builds desire, presents the product, and closes.
**Awareness:** Prob–Prod. A full-length VSL carries the whole Symptom → Problem → Solution → Product → Offer argument (canon §2), so it can open a problem-aware market as well as close a product-aware one.
**Best for:** High-AOV, supplements, health and wellness — anything needing substantial trust before checkout.
**Mechanic:** Reframe opens it; Social Witness sustains it. A VSL runs several mechanics in sequence rather than one.

### Yapper
**Medium:** Video
**What it is:** Single-take, direct-to-camera talking head. One speaker, one space, one unbroken performance — as if the phone were propped up and the camera started. Minor jump cuts only if same space, same speaker, no new footage.
**Awareness:** Any. Pure delivery vehicle; the stage comes from what is said.
**Best for:** Brands with a strong creator or founder voice, and categories where personality-led storytelling converts.
**Mechanic:** Trojan Horse, Social Witness, Reframe.
