# Copy checks — worked before/after examples

Seven worked pairs, each naming the check it illustrates. Load when you want the pattern rather than the rule. Checks live in `copy-checks.md` (priority 1–7) and `copy-checks-standard.md` (8–14 plus the CTA doctrine); the policy gallery is in `copy-policy-risk.md`.

---

## Worked examples

**1 — Invented statistic.**
Before: "9 out of 10 sleep specialists recommend it."
Problem: no substantiation exists in the brand artefact; the number was constructed to sound credible.
Rule: claim substantiation, check 3.
Rewrite: "Two sleep clinicians helped design the strap." (only if the artefact carries it; otherwise cut the credibility line and lead with the mechanism.)

**2 — Hollow superlative.**
Before: "The most comfortable option on the market."
Problem: unsubstantiated superlative with nothing behind it.
Rule: empty claims, check 2.
Rewrite: "Soft-shell padding at the two points everyone else leaves bare."

**3 — Copy that does not match the asset.**
Before: "Perfect for the whole family." Asset shows a single-serve unit.
Problem: the copy promises a capacity the visual contradicts.
Rule: match the asset, check 4.
Rewrite: "One cup, ninety seconds, no cleanup."

**4 — Hook fails the gate.**
Before: "We think you'll love our new formula."
Problem: no audience called out, no benefit implied, no curiosity. Fails all three of canon §1.
Rule: hook present and passing, check 5.
Rewrite: "For anyone whose pre-workout leaves them jittery by 10am."

**5 — Clever over simple.**
Before: "Sole searching? We found it."
Problem: the pun costs a beat and the reader has to reverse-engineer the category.
Rule: simple, not clever, check 6.
Rewrite: "The insole for people who stand for nine hours."

**6 — Transactional CTA.**
Before: "Shop now."
Problem: names the advertiser's outcome, gives no reason to act.
Rule: CTA doctrine `[T p.76]`.
Rewrite: "Click below to get your first box before the weekend."

**7 — Policy-risk pattern.**
Line: "Struggling with hormonal acne in your 30s?"
Flag: RISK (pattern, not a policy citation) — presumes knowledge of the reader's condition and age. Needs a human read against `transparency.meta.com/policies/ad-standards/objectionable-content/privacy-violations-personal-attributes` before launch. Do not treat this note as approval or rejection.
