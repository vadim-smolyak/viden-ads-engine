# Voice Patterns — the living swipe file

Twenty-five templates in ten clusters. Canon §3's Phrasing layer: not frames and not mechanisms, but **containers that already sound like a person on a feed**. Every one was collected from content that stopped a real scroll; none were invented at a desk.

**Reach for this file when a draft has the right tactic and the right trigger and still sounds like an ad.** That is the only reason to open it. Do not start here — a hook built from a template outward tends to serve the template.

**How to use one:** pick the template whose emotional register matches what you are executing, fill the `[bracketed variables]` with the persona's own language from `market-research`, then re-score against canon §1. The container does not carry the golden rules for you; a native-sounding hook that names no audience still fails.

**Tactic tags** are from the `tactics.md` index, **triggers** from `triggers.md`. Where a tag reads "X / Y" the template supports either frame depending on the fill.

This is a collection, not a taxonomy. It will never be complete, and does not need to be.

## Cluster 1 — Confession and gatekeeping
*"I know something you don't, and I'm finally telling you."*

**I have been gatekeeping something that is so niche…**
- Tactic: Exclusivity / Curiosity · Trigger: Curiosity Gap
- *"I have been gatekeeping a hydration product so niche my electrolyte-obsessed friends don't know it exists."*

**Someone once told me [X] and I haven't been the same since**
- Tactic: Storytelling / Confession · Trigger: Curiosity Gap, Pain Agitation
- *"Someone once told me most people are chronically dehydrated and don't know it — and I haven't been the same since."*

**Thank you to everyone who told me that [X]**
- Tactic: Social Proof / Relatability · Trigger: Social Proof/Credibility, Curiosity Gap
- *"Thank you to everyone who told me Loop earplugs would change my life at concerts — you were right and I owe you."*

## Cluster 2 — Confusion and discovery
*"Wait, how did I not know this?"*

**So here's what I don't understand…**
- Tactic: Contrarian / Question · Trigger: Curiosity Gap, Pattern Interrupt
- *"So here's what I don't understand — why are we still buying single-use earplugs when reusable ones sound better?"*

**Apparently I've been living in the Stone Age because what do you mean…**
- Tactic: Relatability / Shocking Statement · Trigger: Pattern Interrupt, Curiosity Gap
- *"Apparently I've been living in the Stone Age because what do you mean you can wear earplugs at a concert and still hear the music clearly?"*

**Who was gonna tell me…**
- Tactic: Relatability / Confession · Trigger: Pain Agitation, Curiosity Gap
- *"Who was gonna tell me the reason I leave concerts with a headache isn't the volume — it's the distortion?"*

## Cluster 3 — Hot takes and contrarian angles
*"Everyone believes X and I'm about to complicate that."*

**My hot take is that [X], but here's [what contradicts it]**
- Tactic: Contrarian / Belief · Trigger: Contrarian/Myth-Busting, Pattern Interrupt
- *"My hot take is that most brands don't have a creative problem — but here's why that makes their creative problem worse."*

**If you want [X]… [unexpected consequence]**
- Tactic: Contrarian / If Then · Trigger: Pattern Interrupt, Curiosity Gap
- *"If you want better ad performance… stop making more ads."*

**[X] don't want you to know this because then [Y]**
- Tactic: Contrarian / Myth Busting · Trigger: Contrarian/Myth-Busting, Urgency/Stakes
- *"Big supplement brands don't want you to know this because then you'd realise you've been buying expensive pee."*
- Caution: the withheld fact has to be real and defensible. Conspiracy framing with nothing behind it is a policy and brand risk.

**If this isn't proof that [bold claim]…**
- Tactic: Social Proof / Bold Claim · Trigger: Social Proof/Credibility, Pattern Interrupt
- *"If this isn't proof that creative strategy is the highest-leverage thing a brand can invest in, I don't know what is."*

**I think it's about time we all just said it…**
- Tactic: Contrarian / Belief · Trigger: Pattern Interrupt, Contrarian/Myth-Busting
- *"I think it's about time we all just said it — UGC that looks like an ad performs like an ad."*

## Cluster 4 — Experience arc and progression
*"I learned the hard way so you don't have to."*

**The first time I did [X] I did [A]. The second time, I knew better.**
- Tactic: Storytelling / Confession · Trigger: Social Proof/Credibility, Pain Agitation
- *"The first time I ran a creative test I tracked clicks. The second time, I knew better."*

**If I had 90 days to [X], here's what I would do**
- Tactic: How To / Authority · Trigger: Aspiration/Desire, Curiosity Gap
- *"If I had 90 days to scale a DTC brand on Meta from zero, here's exactly what I'd do with creative."*

## Cluster 5 — Selective audience and stakes
*"This is specifically for you, and you know who you are."*

**This question is for [hyper-specific qualifier] and only [that audience]…**
- Tactic: Demographic Callout / Challenge · Trigger: Identity Call-Out
- *"This question is for brands spending $300K+ on Meta and only those brands — are you analysing creative performance, or just ROAS?"*

**[X] — that's what everyone on [platform] wants you to believe, but is it actually true?**
- Tactic: Contrarian / Myth Busting · Trigger: Contrarian/Myth-Busting, Pattern Interrupt
- *"If you're not testing ten creatives a week you're done for — that's what everyone on LinkedIn wants you to believe, but is it true?"*

## Cluster 6 — Proximity and category framing
*"Here's the fastest shortcut to understanding what this is."*

**Make the [X] of [Y] — the [brand] of [unexpected category]**
- Tactic: Aspirational / Bold Claim · Trigger: Aspiration/Desire, Pattern Interrupt
- *"Make the Patagonia of dog bags — gear-obsessed, unapologetically premium, built for people who treat their dog like a person."*
- Note: only works when the borrowed brand is unambiguously known to *this* persona. Borrow recognition, not prestige.

## Cluster 7 — Soft signals and casual drops
*"Low-key, no pressure, but pay attention."*

**Just a heads up…**
- Tactic: Warning / Direct Address · Trigger: Urgency/Stakes, Pattern Interrupt
- *"Just a heads up — the earplugs you've been wearing at concerts are distorting the music, not just blocking it."*

**Is there anything better than…**
- Tactic: Aspirational / Relatability · Trigger: Aspiration/Desire, Identity Call-Out
- *"Is there anything better than leaving a concert and being able to hear the next morning?"*

## Cluster 8 — Cultural and trend commentary
*"Something in the world just revealed a bigger truth."*

**That's a [cultural shorthand] indicator**
- Tactic: Contrarian / Statistic · Trigger: Pattern Interrupt, Curiosity Gap
- *"Brands cutting creative budgets right now? That's a recession indicator — and not the kind they think they're avoiding."*
- Note: shelf life is the trend's shelf life. Do not brief this on a long production lead time.

**Talking about [one thing] while showing [something that contradicts it]**
- Tactic: Contrast / Shocking Statement · Trigger: Pattern Interrupt, Contrarian/Myth-Busting
- Format note: the contradiction lives across layers — spoken line against visual or text overlay. Write both halves or it does not exist. Hand the visual half to `creative-concepting`.

## Cluster 9 — Explanation structures
*"Let me tell you exactly why this is the way it is."*

**Want to know why [X]? It's because…**
- Tactic: Explainer / Curiosity · Trigger: Curiosity Gap, Pattern Interrupt
- *"Want to know why your ads stop performing after three weeks? It's because your testing cycle is slower than your messaging goes stale."*

**The way [X] needs to be studied. Because tell me why [X].**
- Tactic: Shocking Statement / Relatability · Trigger: Pattern Interrupt, Curiosity Gap
- *"The way this earplug fits needs to be studied. Because tell me why I can hear the music perfectly but not the crowd."*
- Format note: spoken with genuine disbelief energy. The repetition is the emphasis and invites the viewer into the confusion.

## Cluster 10 — Platform-native containers
*Borrowed cultural formats that carry meaning before a brand touches them. Strongest on TikTok, workable on Reels, weakest on Meta feed where the container is less legible.*

> Holding section. Once it reaches eight to ten patterns, split it into its own reference file rather than letting it swamp the clusters above.

**I hope every [X] finds their [Y]**
- Tactic: Aspirational / Relatability · Trigger: Aspiration/Desire, Identity Call-Out
- Energy: sincere, warm, community-rooting-for-you. Works because it reads as goodwill rather than a pitch.
- *"I hope every chronically tired person finds their Buoy."*

**Today was [bad thing] but at least I'm not [worse thing]**
- Tactic: Relatability / Contrast · Trigger: Pain Agitation, Pattern Interrupt
- Energy: self-deprecating gratitude. The laugh of recognition lands before the brand does.
- *"Today was a three-hour meeting day, but at least I'm not the person who sat through it without earplugs on an open floor plan."*

**The [letter] in my name stands for [word]. Which is why there's no [letter] in my name.**
- Tactic: Humor / Contrarian · Trigger: Pattern Interrupt, Curiosity Gap
- Energy: absurdist and self-aware. The logic loop earns a smile before the message lands.
- *"The H in hydration stands for habit. Which is why there's no H in most people's morning routine."*
- This is the template that required Humor to be defined as a tactic — see `tactics-d-h.md`.

## Update protocol — keep this file alive

Any strategist can add to it. Paste the pattern into a conversation with *"add this to the hook voice patterns"* and it gets appended. Each addition needs all five fields, or it is not usable later:

1. **The raw template**, with `[bracketed variables]` marking what gets filled.
2. **Tactic tag** — from `tactics.md`. If no tactic fits, say so; that is a signal the tactic library has a gap, and a gap gets closed by defining the tactic, never by inventing a tag. Humor got added exactly this way.
3. **Trigger** — from `triggers.md`. Only those eight.
4. **One filled example**, in a real brand's voice.
5. **Cluster placement** — which emotional job does it do? Extend an existing cluster or open a new one with a one-line description.

Add a **format note** where the pattern only works across layers (spoken against visual), and a **caution** where it carries claim or policy risk. Retire a pattern rather than keep it once its cultural container is dead — a stale native format is more obviously an ad than a plain sentence.
