# Hook Tactics — Set A entries A–C

Full entries for the tactics named in `tactics.md`. Read the index first — it answers most lookups on its own. **Never read more than one shard for one hook set.**

**Awareness shorthand:** `Un` unaware · `Prob` problem-aware · `Sol` solution-aware · `Prod` product-aware · `Most` most-aware · `Any` = no stage signal (canon §2). **Triggers** are the eight in `triggers.md`.

---

## A–C

### Aspirational
**Is:** Frames the identity, lifestyle or status the viewer wants. Speaks to who they intend to become, not who they are.
**Not:** Belief — that is the *brand's* stand. This is the *customer's* desired identity.
**Fit:** Un–Sol. Lifestyle, beauty, fitness, status categories.
**Trigger:** Aspiration/Desire
**Ex:** *"Glow like never before."*

### Authority
**Is:** Credibility from expertise, credentials, certification or institutional backing.
**Not:** Social Proof — that runs on volume of people. Authority runs on standing.
**Fit:** Prob–Prod. Health, supplements, skincare, financial. Strongest against sceptics.
**Trigger:** Social Proof/Credibility
**Ex:** *"Dermatologist recommended."*
**Book variant** `[T p.37]`: authority earned by disclosed scale of experience — *"I spent 10 million dollars on Facebook ads… here's the #1 way to scale."* Powerful, and only usable when the number is real and the client will stand behind it (canon §8).

### Belief
**Is:** Opens with the brand's point of view, mission or values. The brand takes a stand.
**Not:** Aspirational — this is the brand's mission, not the customer's identity.
**Fit:** Un–Prob. Brand-building, mission-led products, values-aligned buyers.
**Trigger:** Aspiration/Desire, Contrarian/Myth-Busting
**Ex:** *"We believe skincare should be simple."*

### Bold Claim
**Is:** An outsized, extreme or superlative promise. Stakes a definitive position — including a *singularity* claim ("the only one that works").
**Not:** Exclusivity — that gates *access*. Bold Claim asserts the claim has no equal.
**Fit:** Sol–Prod. Competitive categories where differentiation is hard and moderate claims are ignored.
**Trigger:** Pattern Interrupt, Urgency/Stakes
**Ex:** *"Nothing else comes close."*
**Book variant** `[T p.38]`, pattern 10 — stress the exclusiveness of the claim: *"The only Facebook ads strategy that actually gets results."*

### Call To Action First
**Is:** Opens with an explicit transactional instruction. No build-up.
**Not:** Directive — that shifts mindset or behaviour, not the purchase itself.
**Fit:** Most. Retargeting, sale and offer campaigns.
**Trigger:** Urgency/Stakes
**Ex:** *"Shop the restock."*

### Challenge
**Is:** Competitive framing that dares the viewer to test, attempt or prove something.
**Fit:** Un–Prob, for achievement-oriented identities. Fitness, gaming, performance.
**Trigger:** Identity Call-Out, Pattern Interrupt
**Ex:** *"I bet you can't finish this."*

### Confession
**Is:** A candid admission — brand or person — that earns credibility through vulnerability.
**Not:** Myth Busting — a confession admits *I* was wrong; myth busting says *the market* is wrong.
**Fit:** Prob–Sol. Rebuilding trust, countering scepticism, standing out in polished categories.
**Trigger:** Social Proof/Credibility, Pattern Interrupt
**Ex:** *"I was wrong about sunscreen."*

### Contrast
**Is:** Juxtaposes two things — products, costs, outcomes, identities — to expose a mismatch or a clear winner. Includes rival comparison.
**Not:** Contrarian — that breaks a *belief*. Contrast compares two *things*. And not Claim Before/After, which compares one subject against its own past.
**Fit:** Sol–Prod. Price sensitivity, upgrade messaging, competitive conquesting.
**Trigger:** Pattern Interrupt, Pain Agitation
**Ex:** *"Don't put $5 gas in a $50,000 car."*
**Book variant** `[T p.37]`, pattern 5 — compare the claim to its rival: *"…scales 3× faster than traditional scaling methods."* Any comparative number must be the client's own measurement (canon §8).

### Contrarian
**Is:** Deliberately opposes conventional wisdom, expected advice or what expertise says should be true.
**Not:** Contrast (a comparison, not a rule broken); Myth Busting (corrects a factual error rather than defying accepted advice).
**Fit:** Prob–Sol. Educated, sceptical audiences in categories saturated with conventional advice.
**Trigger:** Contrarian/Myth-Busting, Pattern Interrupt
**Ex:** *"I'm a vet who doesn't take her dog to the vet."*

### Curiosity
**Is:** Opens a loop the viewer needs closed. Withholds exactly enough to compel the next second.
**Fit:** Any. The default when the audience is not yet emotionally invested.
**Trigger:** Curiosity Gap
**Ex:** *"Wallpaper isn't for walls."*
