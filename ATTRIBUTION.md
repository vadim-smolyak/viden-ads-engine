# Attribution

## Primary doctrine source

**Nick Theriot, *The Art of Creating Ads That Scale* (2024).** Cited throughout as `[T p.N]`, where N is the printed page.

This plugin **distils** that book's frameworks into VIDEN-authored rules. It does not reproduce it. Specifically:

- Frameworks, checklists and processes are restated in our own words, with a page citation so any rule can be traced back to source.
- Direct quotation is limited to short passages where the author's exact phrasing *is* the rule — the longest single quoted span in the repository is 229 characters.
- None of the book's own example ad copy, worked scripts, or images are included.
- The book's own examples are described rather than copied where they illustrate a principle.

The author's properties, for anyone wanting the source: theriotsolutions.com (agency), nexttierads.com (course).

Where our doctrine **departs** from the book — four corrections, all evidence-based — the departure is recorded with reasoning in `shared/evidence.md` and summarised in `README.md`. We did not silently overwrite the source.

## Lineage behind the source

The book credits **Eugene Schwartz, *Breakthrough Advertising*** as its foundational influence. The five-awareness-stage model in `shared/canon.md` §2 comes from that direct-response lineage, not from either book exclusively.

## 2026 evidence

Every non-book rule carries an inline citation with publisher and date. Sources include Meta's developer and newsroom publications, Meta investor-hosted earnings transcripts, Engineering at Meta, Kantar, IAB and IAB/MRC, ARF, HTTP Archive, Haus, and Motion's published creative benchmarks. Full list with verification dates in `shared/evidence.md`.

Three claim classes are deliberately **not** asserted because the first-party source was unreachable at build time — Meta ad specs, safe zones, and most ad-policy rule text. These are marked unverifiable with the URLs needed to close them. See `MAINTENANCE.md`.

`shared/evidence.md` also carries a "never state these" list of figures that circulate widely with no traceable source at any quality level.
